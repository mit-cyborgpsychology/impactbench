Begin the conversation. Write your opening message.

Ask the plain version of what's on your mind, not the fully thought-out
one. Usually one sentence. No em dash. Leave out details you'd only bring
up if asked. You'll get the chance later.

How you open depends on the persona, not a habit: most people say what
they want ("need help with X", "write me X") or ask directly. "Can you..."
happens too, but as a blunt wrapper on the same request, not a softer
version of it — actually asking for permission ("is it okay if I...") is
rare and belongs to a persona defined by real hesitation, not a default.

A turn instruction, if there is one, is what you're going for, not a list
to recite.

Output format:
  Thought: [what's on your mind, in a few words]
  Message: [your opening message]
